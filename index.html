WhatsApp Number Ban

Developed by Hussain Bux Brohi

Number ban ho gaya? Ghabraiye mat, ye asli raasta hai.

Koi bhi third-party tool ya website aapka WhatsApp number technically unban nahi kar sakti — ye sirf WhatsApp/Meta khud kar sakte hain. Ye guide aapko wahi official tareeka batati hai jo kaam karta hai.

Ban kyun hota hai?

  • Bohot zyada log aapko block/report kareinEspecially bulk messages ya unknown logon ko messages bhejne par.
  • Third-party ya modified apps ka istemalGB WhatsApp, WhatsApp Plus jaisi apps use karne par account permanently ban ho sakta hai.
  • Automated ya bulk messagingEk hi message bohot logon ko baar baar bhejna spam count hota hai.
  • Naya number, purane ban se linkAgar wo number pehle kisi banned account se juda tha.

Unban karwane ke steps

  1. App khol kar review request karein

    Jab ban screen aaye, us par seedha Request a review ka button hota hai — usi se apeal karein, sabse fast tareeka yahi hai.

  2. Agar button na mile, email karein

    Apna number full country code ke saath (jaise +92...) likh kar support@whatsapp.com par email bhejein aur wajah puchein.

  3. Modified app hai to pehle uninstall karein

    GB WhatsApp ya similar app se ban hua hai to asli WhatsApp install karke hi review maango — warna dobara ban hoga.

  4. Sabar rakhein

    Temporary ban khud hi kuch ghanton se lekar 2-3 din mein clear ho jata hai agar dobara spam-like activity na ho.

Kya karein, kya na karein

YE KAREIN

  • Sirf official WhatsApp app use karein
  • Review request ke baad 24–48 ghante wait karein
  • Sirf apne saved contacts ko message bhejein

YE NA KAREIN

  • "Unban" karne wali koi bhi paid website ya app use na karein — ye sab fraud hain
  • Bar bar naye accounts try na karein, isse number permanently flag ho sakta hai
  • Unknown logon ko bulk messages na bhejein

Aam sawaal

Kya koi website sach mein number unban kar sakti hai?

Nahi. Ban WhatsApp ke apne servers par set hota hai; sirf wahi hata sakte hain. Jo site paisa lekar "unban" ka wada kare, wo scam hai.

Review request ke baad kitna time lagta hai?

Zyada tar cases mein kuch ghante se 24-48 ghante. Kabhi kabhi zyada bhi lag sakta hai — dobara request bar bar na bhejein.

Permanent ban hat sakta hai?

Bohot mushkil hai, khaas kar agar modified app ya spam ki wajah se hua ho. Phir bhi ek honest review request bhejna hi sahi tareeka hai.
